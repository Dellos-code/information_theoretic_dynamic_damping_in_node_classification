## B. Methodology and over-claiming

**B1 (critical for the calibration claim): "EDSP has the lowest NLL on signed graphs" is untested, and the comparison is not fair.**
- **No test exists.** `aggregate_e1.py` reports mean NLL/ECE only; there is no Wilcoxon test on NLL, no CI, and no pre-registered calibration hypothesis.
- **The signed baselines' NLL is set by an arbitrary constant.** `signed_vote` (`edsp/baselines/propagation.py:139`) and `signed_lgc` (`propagation.py:228`) map scores to probabilities as `score − min + 1e-9`, then renormalise. For k = 2 the losing class always gets probability of order 1e-9, so every misclassified node costs −ln(1e-9) ≈ **20.7 nats** (verified on a toy graph: all wrong test nodes give exactly 20.7). Their NLL is therefore about 20.7 × error rate. It measures the 1e-9 constant, not calibration. The report half-acknowledges this ("turn scores into probabilities by a shift") but still presents the ordering as a finding.
- **A one-parameter recalibration removes EDSP's advantage.** Give signed_lgc's hard predictions a constant confidence c (fit on validation; I used c = split accuracy as an upper bound on what the fit can achieve). Its NLL becomes the binary entropy H(acc), which is **≤ EDSP-tuned's NLL on 10 of 11 signed graphs** (wiki-elec is a tie, 0.476 vs 0.475). Examples:

  | graph | signed_lgc (recalibrated) | EDSP-tuned |
  |---|---|---|
  | wiki-editor | 0.397 | 0.412 |
  | wiki-rfa | 0.544 | 0.587 |
  | senate-107 | 0.103 | 0.273 |
  | house-107 | 0.122 | 0.181 |

- **ECE goes the other way.** EDSP-tuned's ECE is **worse** than signed_lgc's on 7 of 8 Congress graphs (e.g. house-107: 0.135 vs 0.029; senate-107: 0.194 vs 0.026; senate-114: 0.089 vs 0.025). On wiki-editor harmonic and harmonic+CMN have lower ECE (0.072 / 0.070 vs 0.080).
- The same mapping problem affects LinBP (`F + 1/k`, clip, renormalise; ECE 0.73–0.79 on Amazon/Coauthor) and majority (one-hot). Calibration comparisons against those methods are not meaningful either.
- **Fix:** before comparing NLL/ECE, put every method on an equal footing with a validation-fitted calibration map, e.g. temperature or Platt scaling on the raw scores for all methods including EDSP, or softmax of scores with a tuned temperature. Then test NLL with the same Wilcoxon + Holm protocol. Until then, drop or explicitly qualify the calibration sentence.

**B2 (major): E2 Friedman headline ranks only against sign-blind methods, and is dominated by 8 non-independent Congress graphs.**
- "EDSP-tuned 1.73" (E2_REPORT.md:74-75) excludes signed_vote and signed_lgc (disclosed).
- Over the 9 ranked signed graphs, with the signed baselines included: **signed_lgc 1.94, signed_vote 2.28, EDSP-tuned 2.67, EDSP-default 3.11** (Friedman p = 8.9e-11, CD = 4.52; no significant difference among the sign-aware methods). EDSP is third, not first.
- 8 of the 11 ranked datasets are Congress chamber-sessions, and the Senate test sets have 19–21 nodes (one node = 5 points). The "ties" with the signed baselines are therefore low-power non-results, and the Friedman/Holm families are dominated by one data source.
- **Fix:** report the 9-graph ranking with signed baselines as the primary E2 ranking, and also report Congress as a single aggregated dataset (or as a random effect).

**B3 (major): The Holm family decides the wiki-editor verdict against signed_lgc.**
- With the A6 family (11 datasets) the result is a tie. With the earlier 3-dataset family it was a significant loss (raw p = 0.0195).
- A6 was written before Congress ran, so "tie" is the pre-registered outcome. The report must say that consistently, not "significant" in §3 (A3).

**B4 (major): H1/H2 verdicts have no pre-registered decision rule.**
- PREREGISTRATION §1 lists outcome labels, but no rule exists for "≈" (H1) or "near chance" (H2); A5 and A7 added rules only for H3 and H4.
- "Supported for harmonic LP" rests on "within 0.4 points" on six hand-picked datasets. The two other C1 datasets are left out, including WikiCS, where EDSP-default is **−3.44 points** below harmonic (Holm-adjusted p < 0.001 within C1).
- Non-significance is used as evidence of equivalence. EDSP-default is in fact significantly different from harmonic on amazon-computers (−0.14, adjusted p = 0.027) and coauthor-physics (−0.09, adjusted p = 0.035); the differences are tiny but significant.
- **Fix:** add an amendment with an equivalence margin (TOST, e.g. ±1 point) and apply it to all C1 datasets; until then label H1/H2 "descriptive, no decision rule".

**B5 (major): Pre-registered H7 items are only partly delivered.**
- **Secondary column missing for EDSP-tuned.** PREREGISTRATION §2 requires the "mean of last two iterates" column "whenever at least one run of that dataset did not converge". `run_e1.py:203-204` computes it only for EDSP-default, so the two oscillating YelpChi EDSP-tuned runs (the headline method) have no secondary column, and their reported AUC depends on the parity of max_iter. The same applies to MIX: 13 non-converged EDSP-tuned runs (citeseer 8, wiki-editor 5), 9 of them flagged oscillating.
- **H7(c) coverage and convergence.** H7(c) promises "for every run"; it is computed on 3 splits, with GS capped at 300 sweeps (documented in SPEC_AMBIGUITIES). The distance is then averaged, which hides the non-converged public splits (A7). The L1 distance between two non-converged iterates says nothing about non-uniqueness of the fixed point.
- **The oscillation flag is very strict.** `core.py:318-323` flags oscillation only when the whole state is period-2 to max-norm < 1e-9. A 2-cycle on some nodes while others are still moving is not flagged, so "No period-2 oscillation was flagged" is weak evidence. H7(b) "fraction of nodes flagged as period-2" is replaced by "fraction still moving" (documented).
- **"The toy 2-cycles … did not appear here" no longer holds for real graphs** in general: E2 YelpChi with H-LEARNED shows them.

**B6 (major): The tuning budget (R6) is not truly equal, and the asymmetry favours EDSP.**
- EDSP-tuned's 9-point grid contains H-LEARNED, which internally runs a further 6-way l2 selection on validation edges (`polarity.py:193-195`). So EDSP gets 9 configs plus a nested validation-driven search; LGC, PPR-LP and LinBP get 9; harmonic, harmonic+CMN and signed_vote get none.
- The E1 report's "B0/B1 Track-A baselines with equal 9-point grids" (E1_REPORT.md:8) is inaccurate. The p0 axis of EDSP's grid is largely inert in Track A (D2/D5: uniform-prior nodes have α = 1 and w = 1/deg for any constant p0).
- This does not affect E1, where EDSP loses, but every E2/MIX win obtained through H-LEARNED (all 81/81 heterogeneous wiki-editor MIX runs) is slightly optimistic.
- **Fix:** count the inner l2 grid in the budget, or give the baselines an equal nested grid. Report the polarity regimes separately, as Master Prompt §2.5 requires.

**B7 (minor; partly acknowledged): MIX scope.**
- The confirmatory run uses fresh re-wiring seeds but the **same first 3 splits** (same train/val/test node sets) as the exploratory run, so it is a partial replication.
- The "logistic (deterministic chaos)" condition assigns logistic-map values to nodes in random order (`perturb.py:79-88`). That is i.i.d. arcsine = Beta(½, ½), i.e. the beta family with m = 0.5, c = 1; it is not a distinct "chaotic" mixing type.
- The generator's locality rule makes per-node targets structurally visible. The report acknowledges this (§3); keep that caveat attached to every MIX win.
- Holm is applied per base graph only (110 tests overall); immaterial given p ≈ 1e-5.

**B8 (minor): Exclusions.**
- Sampson, wiki-rfa, wiki-elec and minesweeper are excluded consistently from every win/tie/loss table, Friedman ranking and C3c/C3d pool (`aggregate_e1.py:32,127`).
- But the wiki-rfa/wiki-elec exclusion is not in PREREGISTRATION §3. It exists only in code and SPEC_AMBIGUITIES, from PR-3 (519ee59), before E2 ran. Add it as an amendment.
- Amendments A5–A7 were inserted above A3 rather than appended, contrary to the file's own append-only rule.

**B9 (minor): Other reporting conventions.**
- "± std" is the population std (`np.std`, ddof = 0).
- The 95 % bootstrap CIs are computed but never reported.
- E1/E2 report no effect sizes alongside the win/tie/loss counts.
- C3d uses an unnormalised Frobenius distance (it grows with k and with class imbalance). Binary, imbalanced `questions` therefore lands in the "structured compatibility" cell, where the complement operator is already exact for k = 2. This weakens H3/C3d, which are E5 topics.
- R7: ogbn-proteins (C4, needed for H5) is listed in E1 as "E2", but E2 neither runs it nor lists it as not run.

## C. Code bugs and risks

No bug was found that changes an E1/E2/MIX accuracy or ROC-AUC conclusion. Test-label isolation holds:
- Only `edsp.eval.evaluate` reads test labels; it is reached via `evaluate`/`stratified_accuracy`, after selection.
- Validation labels are read only through `edsp.selection`.
- Strict vaults ran in production, with zero NOT-RUN records.
- Library code never reads `GraphData._labels` directly.

The baselines and metrics are correct on toy checks. Holm, Friedman (scipy) and Nemenyi (`studentized_range/√2`) are standard and reproduce my independent implementations.

## Verdict

**Safe (numbers reproduce exactly; conclusions follow):**
- **E1:** Track-A EDSP does not beat LGC on any real dataset (0/4/10), and the full win/tie/loss table, Friedman ranks, stratified table and C3c/C3d ranks reproduce. "Non-convergence is rare" (3/153, slow, not oscillating) holds for EDSP-default.
- **E2:** On the 9 ranked signed graphs, EDSP-tuned significantly beats every sign-blind baseline and shows no detectable difference from signed_vote (except a wiki-editor win) or signed_lgc. Results on amazon-fraud (tie with LGC) and yelpchi (loss) also reproduce.
- **MIX:** The exploratory 17 9-of-9 wins and the confirmatory 17 / 10 / 83 verdicts reproduce exactly. The narrow statement (wins only for k = 2 heterophilic/heterogeneous mixing and two or three k = 5 conditions, through H-LEARNED exploiting generator-visible structure) is supported. It still needs the partial-replication (same splits) and nested-tuning caveats.

**Not safe, needs correction:**
1. **Calibration:** "EDSP has the lowest NLL / is better calibrated on signed graphs" is untested and an artefact of the baselines' 1e-9 probability floor. ECE points the other way on Congress. This conclusion is not supported.
2. **H7 for E2:** "no non-convergence and no oscillation on any E2 graph" is false (YelpChi EDSP-tuned 2/10 oscillating). The pre-registered secondary column is missing for those runs. E1's "GS and damped fixed points coincide" is unsupported on the non-converged public splits.
3. **E2 text:**
   - "EDSP-default = EDSP-tuned on signed graphs" is false (house-93 +7.3).
   - "wiki-editor −0.31 significant" contradicts the pre-registered Holm result (tie).
   - "unsigned methods 0–25 % on heterophilic nodes" is false on Congress (LinBP 85.5 %).
   - The rank "EDSP-tuned 1.73" should not be the headline: with the signed baselines it is third.
4. **H1/H2 verdicts:** post hoc (no decision rule; equivalence inferred from non-significance; WikiCS omitted). Relabel them as descriptive.
