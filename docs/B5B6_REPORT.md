# B5 / B6 — EDSP against signed GNNs and fraud GNNs (PREREGISTRATION A17)

This is the "ours vs SOTA" comparison of A17, not a hypothesis test. Every number is read
from `results/aggregates/b5b6/B5B6_SUMMARY.md` / `b5b6.json`, generated by
`scripts/b5b6_compare.py` from:
- the new records in `results/runs/b5` and `results/runs/b6` (`ASSEMBLY.md` in each lists the
  source runs);
- the existing EDSP records: `results/runs/e2` for Track A, and `results/runs/e3` for the
  amazon-fraud Track-B records.

All new records come from commit bdd1ad7. There are no NOT-RUN records.

Statistics: Wilcoxon over splits on the headline metric; Holm across the ranked graphs of the
family, separately for each method; a win or loss requires Holm-adjusted p < 0.05.

## 1. Bottom line

- **B5, signed graphs.** On the 9 ranked graphs (8 Congress graphs and wiki-editor),
  EDSP-tuned against the four signed GNNs:

  | | Win | Tie | Loss |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | SGCN | 1 | 5 | 3 |
  | SNEA | 2 | 7 | 0 |
  | SDGNN | 1 | 8 | 0 |
  | SiGAT | 3 | 4 | 2 |
  | **Total (36 comparisons)** | **7** | **24** | **5** |

  - **EDSP is #1 by mean on 1 of the 9 graphs (congress-senate-100).** Even there it is only
    tied with SGCN, the strongest method overall.
  - **Exploratory average ranks** (not preregistered) among the 7 label-only methods: SGCN 2.56,
    SiGAT 3.56, signed LGC 3.83, signed vote 4.17, **EDSP-tuned 4.39**, SDGNN 4.44, SNEA 5.06.
    Friedman p = 0.275, so these ranks do not differ significantly.
- **Where EDSP wins:**
  - **wiki-editor, against all four GNNs** (+4.0 to +14.5 points). But signed LGC, a simple E2
    baseline, has a higher mean there (86.43 vs 86.12), and E2 found EDSP tied with it. This
    win belongs to propagation on the given signs in general, not to EDSP in particular.
  - **The Senate graphs of the 100th / 107th Congress, against SiGAT** (+6.0 / +4.7), **and
    the 107th against SNEA** (+10.5).
- **Where EDSP loses:** the House graphs of the 93rd / 100th Congress against SGCN and SiGAT
  (−3.8 to −8.8 points), and the 114th House against SGCN (−1.6).
- **B6, fraud graphs** (Track B, ROC-AUC; EDSP-B-tuned):

  | | amazon-fraud | yelpchi |
  |---|---|---|
  | vs CARE-GNN | win (+1.44) | loss (−2.31) |
  | vs PC-GNN | tie (+0.25) | loss (−10.53) |
  | rank among the 8 methods shown | 3rd | 6th |
  | best method | MLP 96.97 | PC-GNN 87.39 |

**Verdict.** The signed and fraud GNNs do not change the overall picture of
`docs/FINAL_REPORT.md`: on real graphs EDSP is not #1 against the strongest comparator. The
only consistent advantage over the GNNs is on wiki-editor, and it is shared with simple signed
propagation.

Reading:
- **Congress House vs Senate.**
  - On the House graphs (about 440 nodes, 89 test nodes per split), SGCN and SiGAT are
    significantly better than EDSP on the 93rd and 100th Congress.
  - On the Senate graphs (about 100 nodes) the GNNs vary more and EDSP wins three comparisons.
  - With 10 splits and Holm over 9 graphs, a mean gap of 2–4 points is often a tie.
- **Case studies (wiki-rfa, wiki-elec).** All four GNNs are 8.5–17.4 points above EDSP, with
  raw p = 0.002 (all ten splits in the same direction). There the label is decided by the
  node's incoming votes (`docs/E2_REPORT.md`, §3.1 C5.3). A label-free link-sign embedding
  encodes each node's sign balance; propagation from labelled neighbours does not. These graphs
  are not ranked and were not ranked in E2; they show what EDSP does not capture, not a fair
  benchmark.
- **Attribution.** On the Congress graphs and wiki-editor, EDSP-tuned's wins and ties track
  signed vote and signed LGC (E2: 1 / 8 / 0 and 0 / 9 / 0). On all four House graphs the best
  GNN has a higher mean than every propagation method, not only EDSP (only the comparisons with
  EDSP are tested).

still holds under the Holm correction used there, but not on the raw p-value. The final report
now says so.

## 4. Caveats

- **Signed GNNs (B5).**
  - They are used as label-free link-sign embeddings plus logistic regression. This is the
    library's own setting, not end-to-end node classification.
  - The library's default hyperparameters are used, with a 9-point grid. This is the same
    budget as EDSP-tuned.
  - Our graphs are undirected, so the direction-specific parts of SDGNN and SiGAT carry no
    information (A17, `docs/SPEC_AMBIGUITIES.md`).
  - All of this can only weaken the GNNs, i.e. it favours EDSP.
- **Fraud GNNs (B6).**
  - CARE-GNN and PC-GNN are re-implementations, run under this repository's protocol: 40/20/40
    splits, early stopping on validation AUC, at most 500 epochs.
  - Parity with the published numbers has not been checked (TO VERIFY).
  - YelpChi uses the 32-dimensional features shipped with CARE-GNN.
- **Not run:** GAGA and H²-FDetector (B6).
